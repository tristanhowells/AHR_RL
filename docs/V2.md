# V2 environment and agent

V2 is the environment and agent redesigned around what the data showed. The
objective is unchanged: $500 per race, back and/or lay any runner at any
available price and stake, subject to the Betfair funds check, and be green
before the market turns in-play.

## What the tests established

The tests used 1,185 AU races (27 Aug – 26 Sep 2026), split by date into
train, validation and test.

| Test | Finding | Consequence for V2 |
|---|---|---|
| Baselines | Do nothing $0; random −$7/race; passive rule scalper −$0.69/race | $0 is the bar to beat |
| Edge test (10–60s) | Crossing the spread costs ~4.3% per round trip; no model-selected rule beats it | No quick cross-spread scalping |
| Feature study | WoM is the strongest signal (correlation 0.18, same sign in 99% of races); engineered features add a little predictive power, no profit | Keep them as inputs; don't expect them alone to find an edge |
| Signal bot + fill modes | The passive edge-test profit exists only if any trade at your price fills you completely. It is negative with realistic queues (−$0.29 to −$0.80/race) and ≤ $0 even when always first in the queue | Short-horizon passive scalping is ruled out |
| Cross-matching | Virtual prices beat the real best price in only 0.4–2% of snapshots | Runner-by-runner fills are fine |
| Early PPO runs | Collapse to do-nothing without an exploration floor; per-runner credit and bracket actions are needed | Keep those; add an exploration floor |

The one route not yet ruled out is **longer holds**. The 2–8 minute moves into
the off are larger than the round-trip cost, if they can be predicted. V2 is
built for that route. It also keeps the agent able to take shorter trades,
in case combining signals helps.

## Design

**Environment** (`EnvConfig.v2()`):
- Decisions every 10s (≈60 per race instead of ≈300), which makes credit
  assignment easier.
- A bracket's take-profit can be 3 ticks, 8 ticks or 0. 0 means hold until
  the auto-green at the scheduled start, with a 12-tick stop. Maximum hold
  is 10 minutes.
- Everything else is unchanged: the realistic simulator (queue position,
  latency, liquidity already taken), the funds check, commission,
  scratchings, the auto-green safety net and per-runner reward.

**Inputs per runner** (`features.runner_features_v2`):
- the 38 v1 features;
- 8 engineered order-book features (best-level WoM, WoM change over 10s and
  30s, trade flow over 10s and 30s, session VWAP, LTP vs 30s VWAP, 2-minute
  volume);
- 2-minute, 5-minute and since-open price moves;
- 17 catalogue form/race features (`catalogue.py`);
- the forecaster's predictions.

**Forecaster** (`forecaster.py`): gradient-boosted models of the price move
over each horizon available in the edge samples (10s up to 480s). They are
fitted on train days only and cross-fitted by day, so every prediction the
agent sees is out-of-sample, on train races as well as on val/test races.
Supervised learning finds a weak signal far more efficiently than RL does;
RL then only has to learn what to do with it.

**Agent** (PPO, `train.py --env v2`):
- runner-equivariant transformer with factorised actions and per-runner
  credit;
- an **exploration floor**: a minimum chance of opening a trade (e.g. 5%),
  mixed into the policy so PPO stays on-policy and reduced to 0 over the
  first half of training;
- an extra entropy bonus on the trade/no-trade decision.

**Evaluation** (`report.py`):
- test-set results compared with baselines;
- a breakdown by race type, liquidity and field size;
- `gate.json`.

The live bot refuses real money unless the gate passed: mean > 0 and t > 2
across test races.

**Live** (`live/session.py`, `live/bot.py`): the same code path as training.
It fetches the catalogue from `list_market_catalogue` and uses the full
forecaster. The v1 and v2 parity tests assert identical observations and P&L
when replaying recordings.

## What to expect

V2 gives the agent every input and tool the tests suggest might matter. It
does not create an edge. If the long-horizon edge test and V2's test report
both come back ≤ $0, doing nothing is the correct policy for this data, and
the next step is new information, not more RL.

If a single segment looks positive (e.g. high-liquidity thoroughbred races),
treat that as a hypothesis. Confirm it on races recorded after the run before
trading it, because it wasn't chosen in advance.
